Actors

ActorDescription
Registered User (Trader)A user with an account who trades virtually, manages a watchlist, and reviews portfolio and AI insights.
AdministratorManages user accounts, reviews audit logs, and monitors AI model performance.
Public VisitorAn unauthenticated visitor who can browse the public market summary without an account.
